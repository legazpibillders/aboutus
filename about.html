About Us

Who We Are

Building reliable infrastructure for a stronger tomorrow

Legaspi Billders Construction is a sole proprietorship engaged in civil, structural, and electromechanical services across the Philippines. Since its establishment in 2020, the company has supported the growth of infrastructure, telecommunications, and commercial developments in both private and government sectors.

With operations in Bicol Region, Metro Manila, and North Luzon, we continue to deliver dependable construction services rooted in safety, efficiency, and professionalism. Our work spans telecommunication infrastructure, general construction, power-related systems, residential and commercial developments, and design-and-build solutions.

Why choose us

  • Skilled workforce and experienced technical teams
  • Focus on safety, quality, and turnaround efficiency
  • Capability to handle both private and government projects
  • Trusted support from planning to completion
